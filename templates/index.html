About the Project

Empowering Engineers with Data-Driven Career Intelligence

Developed for Smart India Hackathon & Final-Year Engineering Innovation, the Intelligent Expertise System addresses the critical gap between academic training and industry tech domain expectations.

By leveraging Machine Learning algorithms (Random Forest, Decision Tree, Naive Bayes), our platform provides objective, quantifiable clarity on where a developer stands and how to reach their target career goals.
